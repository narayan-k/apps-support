Scene

Privacy policy

Scene keeps your game progress on your device and does not require an account. It uses Google services to request consent where needed and to show ads in Endless and Zen.

Effective and last updated: 6 October 2026

Information Scene stores

Your gameplay progress and statistics—including scores, completed scenes, difficulty progress and your current Daily challenge record—are stored locally on your device. Scene does not send this gameplay information to Narayan Kuleindiren or maintain a server-side copy.

You can remove locally stored information by deleting the app from your device.

Accounts

Scene does not require or offer user accounts. You do not provide a name, email address or password to play.

Advertising

Scene uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show interstitial advertisements. Ads may appear in Endless after completed runs and in Zen after completed scenes. Daily is ad-free.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device information, IP address, advertising identifiers, interactions with ads, diagnostic information and your consent choices. They may use this information to deliver advertising, measure ad performance, and detect or prevent fraud and abuse. The exact information used depends on your device settings, region and consent choices.

Scene does not control how Google or its advertising partners process information. Read Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s information about display advertising for more detail.

Consent and privacy choices

Scene uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to request and record consent where required. Google determines whether a consent message or privacy-options control is required for you.

When required, you can revisit your choices from the Privacy choices control on Scene’s home screen. You can also use privacy and advertising controls provided by iOS and Google.

This website

This website does not use analytics, tracking technologies, cookies, advertising or forms. If you follow a link to another website, that website’s privacy practices apply.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Scene’s features or service providers change. The date at the top of the page will show the latest revision.
