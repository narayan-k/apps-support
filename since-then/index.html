Reminders

Check-in reminders are optional and use local iOS notifications. If notifications are blocked, allow them in Settings → Notifications → Since Then.

Widgets

Add Since Then from the iOS widget gallery. To choose a different tracker, touch and hold the Home Screen widget, then choose Edit Widget.

Since Then Pro

If an earlier purchase is not recognised, open the upgrade screen and choose Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account used to buy it.

Privacy and analytics

Tracker details stay on your device. Optional product analytics are off until you choose to share them and can be changed in Privacy & Analytics.
