Since Then

Privacy policy

Your trackers stay on your device. Since Then has no accounts or advertising. You can choose whether to share limited, anonymous product analytics.

Effective and last updated: 6 October 2026

Information stored on your device

Since Then stores your tracker details locally using storage shared by the app and its widgets. This includes tracker names and icons, start and setback dates, check-ins, goals, savings information, reminder settings, purchase status and your analytics choice.

This information is used to calculate and display your progress, update widgets, schedule reminders and remember app settings. It is not sent to Narayan Kuleindiren. Since Then does not require or offer an account, and it does not provide cloud backup or cross-device syncing for tracker data.

Deleting the app removes its locally stored data. Because there is no server-side copy, deleted tracker data cannot be recovered by the developer.

Optional product analytics

Since Then uses PostHog Product Analytics only if you explicitly choose Share product analytics. Choosing No thanks keeps analytics off. You can change this at any time from the app’s menu under Privacy & Analytics.

When analytics are enabled, PostHog receives a random installation identifier and limited information about how the app is used. This may include:

  • app and analytics-library version information;
  • fixed screen names, such as tracker creation, reminders or tracker editing;
  • events such as opening the app, creating or deleting a tracker, completing a check-in, recording a setback or reaching a milestone;
  • broad streak ranges rather than exact streak values;
  • whether optional goal or savings features are enabled, without their values; and
  • notification-permission status.

PostHog may process technical information needed to receive an internet request, including an IP address. Since Then asks PostHog not to use the IP address for location enrichment and limits the event properties it sends.

Information not included in analytics

Since Then does not send tracker names, icons or other text you enter; exact tracker, check-in or setback dates; exact streaks; habit identifiers; savings or spending amounts; currency; email address; contacts; location properties; or Apple advertising and vendor identifiers.

Session recording, automatic interaction capture, crash and error capture, surveys, advertising tracking and person profiles are disabled. Analytics are not used to follow you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Changing your analytics choice

Turning analytics off stops future app and widget events. It does not delete events already delivered to PostHog. To ask about access to or deletion of existing analytics data, contact nkuleindiren@gmail.com. Because the identifier is anonymous, you may need to provide information from the app to help locate a record.

PostHog processes analytics information as a service provider. Read PostHog’s privacy policy for more information about its practices.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Since Then asks iOS for permission to send notifications and schedules them locally on your device. The app does not use remote marketing notifications. You can change notification permission in iOS Settings.

Purchases

Since Then Pro is purchased through Apple’s App Store using StoreKit. Apple processes the purchase and provides the app with verified entitlement information; Since Then does not receive your payment-card details. Apple’s handling of purchase information is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

This website

This website does not use analytics, tracking technologies, cookies, advertising or forms. If you follow a link to another website, that website’s privacy practices apply.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Since Then’s features or service providers change. The date at the top of the page will show the latest revision.

Contact
