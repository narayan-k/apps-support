A quiet logic puzzle for iPhone

Untangle

A tangle of connected dots. Drag them until no lines cross. A quiet logic puzzle with smooth, direct movement and three ways to play.

Coming soon on the App Store See the modes

Three ways to play

Drag. Untangle. Unwind.

Daily
One shared puzzle each day, refreshed at 00:00 UTC. Solve it, then view or share your result. Daily is always ad-free.
Levels
Numbered puzzles that become progressively more challenging. Your unlocked levels and best results are saved on your device.
Zen
An endless sequence that adapts its difficulty to how you play, with progress saved on your device.
