Untangle: Dots & Lines

Privacy policy

Untangle keeps your game progress on your device and does not require an account. It uses Google services to manage privacy choices and show ads in Levels and Zen. Daily is ad-free.

Effective and last updated: 14 October 2026

Game progress

Untangle: Dots & Lines is an iOS puzzle game developed by Narayan Kuleindiren. Your puzzle positions, moves, solving time, Daily results and completed dates, unlocked levels and best results, Zen difficulty and records, statistics, unfinished games and appearance preferences are stored locally on your device.

Untangle does not upload these game records to a developer-operated server. It does not offer cloud synchronisation or online leaderboards.

You can remove these local records by deleting the app and its data from your device. Copies retained through device backups are controlled by your backup settings. Deleting the app does not delete information already processed by third-party services.

Accounts and app permissions

Untangle does not require or offer user accounts. You do not provide a name, email address or password to play. It does not have purchases or a separate product analytics integration.

Untangle does not request access to your precise location, camera, photos, contacts or microphone. Google's advertising services may still estimate approximate location from your IP address as described below.

Advertising

Untangle uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show interstitial advertisements. Ads may appear at continuation points after completed puzzles in Levels and Zen, rather than while you are solving. Daily puzzles do not display ads.

Google and its advertising partners may process device and advertising identifiers, IP addresses and IP-derived approximate location, advertising data, interactions with ads and the app, consent choices, and diagnostic and performance information. This information may be used for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention and service operation. What is processed depends on your consent choices, system permissions, region and the advertising configuration.

This processing is separate from the game records Untangle stores locally. For Google's handling of information, read Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent and privacy choices

Untangle uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and record privacy choices where required. Google's consent system determines whether a consent message or privacy-options control is required for you. Where required, a consent message is shown before ads are requested.

When required, you can revisit your choices using the Privacy choices control on Untangle's home screen. You can also use privacy and advertising controls provided by iOS and Google. You can continue playing when ads cannot be requested or loaded.

Sharing a Daily result

Sharing is optional and initiated by you. The shared text contains the puzzle date, moves, solving time and Untangle's App Store link. It does not contain the puzzle solution or an account identifier.

The app or destination you choose handles the shared information under its own terms and privacy policy.

This website

This website does not use analytics, tracking technologies, cookies, advertising or forms. If you follow a link to another website, that website's privacy practices apply.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Untangle's features or service providers change. The date at the top of the page will show the latest revision.
